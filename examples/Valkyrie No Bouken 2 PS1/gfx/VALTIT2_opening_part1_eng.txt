  Long, long ago......
  The people of Marvel Land had
eternal life.
  But life filled the earth,
and hunger and war followed.
  Seeing this, Heaven built a clock
giving all life a limited span.
   They drove the evil that corrupted
their hearts into the rift of time,
and sealed it away with the Key of Time.
  With life now limited,
people began to cherish it,
and kindness grew in their hearts.
Watched over by the clock tower,
everyone lived together in peace.
