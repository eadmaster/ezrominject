  One day, a man, fearing death,
pulled out the Key of Time.
  This brought a great calamity upon
all of Marvel Land.
  Zouna, the incarnation of evil
sealed in the rift, awoke once more.
  Zouna took the Key of Time
and forced the rift open.
  Then the evil that had been trapped
there for many years was unleashed
upon all of Marvel Land.
  Fear and despair filled the land.
  People and creatures fell one by one,
while others were consumed by evil
and turned into monsters.
